Celebrate with us

Festivals Through the Year

We celebrate the major traditional Chinese festivals with our seniors, children and families. Activities change a little each year – sometimes seniors and children celebrate separately, sometimes all together.

January – February

Lunar New Year & Lantern Festival

We welcome the new year together by making dumplings and sweet rice balls (tangyuan), with festive food, cultural activities and games for seniors and children.

Around June

Dragon Boat Festival

Zongzi (sticky rice dumpling) making. In past years we have joined with a local Chinese school for a festival fair with lantern riddles, games and a charity stall.

September – October

Mid-Autumn Festival

A hands-on snow-skin mooncake workshop – children fill, mould and box their own mooncakes to take home and share.

December

Christmas Celebration

An end-of-year community gathering at our Hornsby centre, led by our Table Tennis Club and supported by AACA. Families are welcome to bring a plate to share.

Once a term

Birthday Celebrations

At the last session of each school term, the Chinese Seniors Association celebrates the birthdays of everyone born that term – with a big cake, good food and plenty of fun, provided by AACA.

Arts & culture

Arts & Cultural Appreciation

Working with our arts partners, AACA brings traditional Chinese arts to the community. Past events have included a guqin (Chinese zither) appreciation session.

We are planning more sessions in calligraphy, embroidery and Chinese painting. Details will be shared here as dates are confirmed.

In loving memory

Saturday Crochet Club – Remembering Ms Sun Chen

For many years, the late Ms Sun Chen volunteered every Saturday afternoon to teach children to crochet. Together they made small crocheted animals, which were regularly donated to the Hornsby Hospital gift shop to raise funds for the hospital.

Since Ms Sun’s passing, the class has been paused. We remember her with deep gratitude – her patience, skill and generosity touched many young people and our wider community.

Can you help carry on her work?

We would love to restart the Saturday Crochet Club. If you can crochet and are available on Saturday afternoons, please register your interest. We still have yarn and materials ready to use.
