How to join AACA

Become a Member

AACA is an incorporated association run by its members. Everyone who supports AACA's work is welcome to apply.

How to join

  1. Apply. Email us to apply for membership. We will send you the membership application form.
  2. Committee decision. Our committee considers each application, and we will let you know the outcome.
  3. Pay the membership fee. We will tell you the current fee and how to pay.

As a member you can

  • Receive notice of general meetings, including our Annual General Meeting
  • Vote at general meetings, including electing the committee
  • Stand for election to the committee

Good to know

Do I need to be a member to join a program? No. You can take part in our programs and classes, or enrol your child, without becoming a member.

Leaving. You can resign at any time by writing to the secretary.

New constitution. Our members adopted a new constitution on 5 August 2026, and it has been lodged with NSW Fair Trading. Once it is registered, the membership fee will be $2 a year, and we will update this page.
