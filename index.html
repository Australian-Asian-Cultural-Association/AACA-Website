Welcome to AACA

Building Connections, Supporting Families, Enriching Communities

AACA is a community organisation supporting individuals and families from Asian cultural backgrounds through community engagement, education, cultural participation and family wellbeing initiatives.

Chinese Seniors Association

AACA's longest-running ongoing community program. Weekly social and wellbeing activities for older community members, Wednesdays 9am – 12pm during NSW school terms. Running since 2008 and part of AACA since 2016.
