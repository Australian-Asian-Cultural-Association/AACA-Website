Privacy

How AACA handles your personal information.

AACA respects your privacy and handles personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

We only collect the personal information you choose to give us – for example your name, email address and phone number when you contact us, register your interest or join an activity.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to you, run our programs and events, and keep you informed about AACA activities. We do not sell or share your information with anyone outside AACA unless you agree or the law requires it.

Photos

We only publish photos of people with their permission, and with a parent or guardian’s permission for children.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or remove your information, or stop receiving messages from us, at any time.

For privacy questions, please email admin@aacansw.org.au.
