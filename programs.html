Chinese Seniors Association

AACA's longest-running ongoing community program. Weekly senior activities and social engagement that bring older community members together through social, cultural and wellbeing activities. First formed in 2008, the group joined AACA in 2016.

WhenWednesdays, 9am – 12pm
NSW school terms only (no sessions in school holidays)
Where703/88-90 George Street, Hornsby
